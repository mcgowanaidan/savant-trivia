How it works
Head-to-head matches — two teams face off each week across 10 categories. Each team gets a question and 30 seconds to answer.
Steal the point — if a team gets it wrong, the other team gets a chance to steal.
Power-ups — Double, Phone a Friend, and Double Dip can swing a match.
Playoffs — top half of the league advances to single-elimination, seeded by record. Points for breaks ties.
